Help

The goal

You are desperate for a toilet. Somewhere in this maze-like shopping mall there is one that works. Find it and press E at its blue door before your Urgency meter reaches 100%.

Controls

W A S DWalk (arrow keys work too)
MouseLook around. Click the game to lock the pointer.
Shift (hold)Sprint — faster, but Urgency rises twice as fast
Space (hold)Clench — stand still; Urgency stops rising while you have Clench stamina
EUse a toilet door
MSound on / off (remembered for next time)
EscPause

On a phone or tablet

Touch controls appear automatically. Turning your phone sideways gives the best view.

Left thumbPut it down anywhere on the left side and drag to walk
Right thumbDrag anywhere else to look around
RUNTap to switch sprinting on or off (it turns red when on)
HOLDHold to clench
USEUse a toilet door — it glows when you're close enough
❚❚Pause
🔊Tap to turn sound on or off. On iPhone, also check the silent switch.

Urgency and Clench

  • Urgency starts at 20% and keeps rising. Sprinting doubles the rate.
  • The food court smells amazing. That's bad: Urgency rises faster while you're in it.
  • Clench stamina drains while you hold Space and slowly refills when you let go.
  • Listen to your body: footsteps speed up above 75%, your stomach starts rumbling above 50%, and above 90% you'll hear your heartbeat.
  • Above 75% the world starts to wobble. Above 90%… hurry.

Stars

Under 1 minute: ★★★ · Under 2 minutes: ★★☆ · Otherwise: ★☆☆

Tips

  • Toilets hide at dead ends and along corridor edges, never near the entrance.
  • Use the fountain in the middle and the food court as landmarks.
  • Save Clench for when you're nearly there.

Troubleshooting

  • The mouse doesn't turn the camera: click inside the game. If your browser blocks pointer lock, click and drag instead.
  • It's slow: close other tabs and make sure hardware acceleration is turned on in your browser settings.
  • No sound: browsers only allow sound after you click, so it starts when you press Play. Check that it isn't muted (press M) and that the tab isn't muted.
  • Blank screen: your browser needs WebGL. Try a recent Chrome, Firefox or Safari.
